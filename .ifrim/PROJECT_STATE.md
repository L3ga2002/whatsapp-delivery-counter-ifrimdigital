## Product Direction

- Desktop app for Windows clients.
- Must import WhatsApp `.zip` and `.txt` exports.
- Must scan exact selected date/time interval.
- Must count courier `ridicat` and `livrat` messages.
- Must support persistent restaurants, couriers, WhatsApp aliases and phone-number-to-name aliases.
- Must show mismatch/review rows before export.
- Must support flexible export: complete report, individual restaurant, selected restaurants, global couriers, review, and work hours.
